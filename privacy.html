Privacy notice for the quote request form

Last updated November 10, 2026. This notice covers quote.floorsgalore.com and our text message program. The full site policy for floorsgalore.com is at floorsgalore.com/privacy-policy.

What we collect

When you submit the quote request form we collect your name, mobile phone number, email address, garage size, anything you type into the form, the page you arrived from, and the ad or campaign code in the link you used.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your quote request, schedule and remind you about estimate appointments, send quotes and project updates, and, if you opted in, send occasional offers. We store it in our customer relationship system (HighLevel) and our scheduling tools.

SMS opt-in

Consent to receive text messages is collected through a separate, unchecked checkbox on the form and is optional. We record the date, time and page of your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to any message.

No sharing of SMS opt-in data

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with, sold to, or rented to any third party or affiliate for their marketing or promotional purposes. We share your information only with service providers who deliver our messages, host our systems, or process data on our behalf, and only for that purpose.

Cookies and tracking

This page may use standard analytics and advertising measurement tools, including Google Analytics and the Meta pixel, to understand how visitors reach us and whether our ads work. These tools use cookies or similar identifiers. You can limit them through your browser settings.

Data security

Your information is stored in access-controlled systems and transmitted over encrypted connections. Only staff who need it to handle your request can see it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or unsubscribe from texts or emails at any time. Call 910-939-5936 or reply STOP to any text message.
